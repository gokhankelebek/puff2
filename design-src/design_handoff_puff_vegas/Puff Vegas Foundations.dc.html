06

Motion — "the tick"

One second, forever. The clock advances with a hard 1s step and no easing on the seconds digit. Every other duration in the system is a subdivision of that beat. Nothing moves at an arbitrary number.
The beat
1000msthe tick · steps(1)
500mspage transition
250msstandard
125msmicro · hover, tap
Thermal easing · hover me
Heats in 320ms, cools over 900ms
The asymmetry is the whole trick. Real embers cool slower than they light, and that mismatch gives the interface thermal mass.
Smoke governance
Never render smoke as smoke. Smoke is a verb here, never a noun.
One ambient effect per viewport, maximum.
Total motion budget 4ms/frame.
Ember particles spawn only from what the user is touching.
Everything degrades to a static gradient under prefers-reduced-motion.
07

The rules

Ember discipline
Ember is the only saturated hue above 2% of screen area. If two things on a screen are ember, one of them is wrong.
Signal discipline
Live-green appears exclusively in the status module. It is never a button, a badge, or a price.
Quarantine
Cedar lives in the humidor. No viewport may carry cedar and ember above 5% combined area; cross-links go through neutral cards.
The constants
The clock, the delivery module, the cash-on-delivery badge, the wordmark and the grain are byte-identical everywhere. They carry the coherence.
Never
Countdown timers · carousels · "3 people are viewing this" · fake low stock · pre-checked add-ons · newsletter modals · a full-screen age overlay.
The floor
Hours, address, phone and directions work with zero JavaScript. The clock hydrates after paint into a fixed-width, tabular placeholder so it can never shift layout.
Everything we sell is a way of making light.
